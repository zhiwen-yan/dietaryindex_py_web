======================================================================
                饮食指数（Dietary Index）原始文献 / 开山之作
        基于 dietaryindex R包 (v2.1) 中各饮食指数函数整理
======================================================================

说明：
  - 对于每个饮食指数，列出构建该指数的那一篇"开山之作"（首创/奠基性论文）。
  - 如该指数有多版本更新，也一并列出对应的更新论文。
  - 引用信息来源于 dietaryindex 包 vignette 中的参考文献链接及PubMed检索。


----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HEI (Healthy Eating Index) — 健康饮食指数
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  【开山之作】HEI-1995（首个HEI）
  Kennedy ET, Ohls J, Carlson S, Fleming K.
  "The Healthy Eating Index: design and applications."
  J Am Diet Assoc. 1995;95(10):1103-1108.
  doi: 10.1016/S0002-8223(95)00300-2

  【HEI-2015 更新版】（本包HEI2015函数实现）
  Krebs-Smith SM, Pannucci TE, Subar AF, et al.
  "Update of the Healthy Eating Index: HEI-2015."
  J Acad Nutr Diet. 2018;118(9):1591-1602.
  doi: 10.1016/j.jand.2018.05.021

  【HEI-2020 更新版】（本包HEI2020函数实现）
  Shams-White MM, Pannucci TE, Lerman JL, et al.
  "Healthy Eating Index-2020: Review and Update Process to Reflect
   the Dietary Guidelines for Americans, 2020-2025."
  J Acad Nutr Diet. 2023;123(9):1280-1288.
  doi: 10.1016/j.jand.2023.05.015


----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. AHEI (Alternative Healthy Eating Index) — 替代健康饮食指数
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  【开山之作】AHEI-2002
  McCullough ML, Feskanich D, Stampfer MJ, et al.
  "Diet quality and major chronic disease risk in men and women:
   moving toward improved dietary guidance."
  Am J Clin Nutr. 2002;76(6):1261-1271.
  doi: 10.1093/ajcn/76.6.1261

  【AHEI-2010 更新版】（本包AHEI函数实现）
  Chiuve SE, Fung TT, Rimm EB, et al.
  "Alternative dietary indices both strongly predict risk of
   chronic disease."
  J Nutr. 2012;142(6):1009-1018.
  doi: 10.3945/jn.111.157222


----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. AHEIP (Alternative Healthy Eating Index for Pregnancy)
    — 孕期替代健康饮食指数
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  【开山之作】
  Rifas-Shiman SL, Rich-Edwards JW, Kleinman KP, Oken E, Gillman MW.
  "Dietary quality during pregnancy varies by maternal characteristics
   in Project Viva: a US cohort."
  J Am Diet Assoc. 2009;109(6):1004-1011.
  doi: 10.1016/j.jada.2009.03.001


----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DASH (Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension)
    — 终止高血压膳食疗法（指数）
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  【开山之作】DASH 临床试验
  Appel LJ, Moore TJ, Obarzanek E, et al.
  "A clinical trial of the effects of dietary patterns on blood
   pressure."
  N Engl J Med. 1997;336(16):1117-1124.
  doi: 10.1056/NEJM199704173361601

  【DASH 饮食评分】（本包DASH函数实现）
  Fung TT, Chiuve SE, McCullough ML, et al.
  "Adherence to a DASH-style diet and risk of coronary heart
   disease and stroke in women."
  Arch Intern Med. 2008;168(7):713-720.
  doi: 10.1001/archinte.168.7.713


----------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DASHI (DASH Index — 基于营养素)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  【开山之作】DASHI 评分方法
  Mellen PB, Gao SK, Vitolins MZ, Goff DC Jr.
  "Deteriorating dietary habits among adults with hypertension:
   DASH dietary accordance, NHANES 1988-1994 and 1999-2004."
  Arch Intern Med. 2008;168(3):308-314.
  doi: 10.1001/archinternmed.2007.119

  注：DASHI基于 Appel et al. 1997 (NEJM) DASH试验的营养素目标，
       由 Mellen et al. 2008 将其标准化为可评分指标。


----------------------------------------------------------------------
6. MED / aMED (Alternate Mediterranean Diet Score)
    — 替代地中海饮食评分
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  【开山之作】地中海饮食评分（MDS）
  Trichopoulou A, Kouris-Blazos A, Wahlqvist ML, et al.
  "Diet and overall survival in elderly people."
  BMJ. 1995;311(7018):1457-1460.
  doi: 10.1136/bmj.311.7018.1457

  【aMED 美国版】（本包MED函数实现）
  Fung TT, Rexrode KM, Mantzoros CS, et al.
  "Mediterranean diet and incidence of and mortality from coronary
   heart disease and stroke in women."
  Circulation. 2009;119(8):1093-1100.
  doi: 10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.108.816736


----------------------------------------------------------------------
7. MEDI (Mediterranean Diet Index — 基于份数/PREDIMED)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  【开山之作】14项地中海饮食依从性筛查工具（MEDAS）
  Schröder H, Fitó M, Estruch R, et al.
  "A short screener is valid for assessing Mediterranean diet
   adherence among older Spanish men and women."
  J Nutr. 2011;141(6):1140-1145.
  doi: 10.3945/jn.110.135566

  【PREDIMED 试验验证】
  Estruch R, Ros E, Salas-Salvadó J, et al.
  "Primary prevention of cardiovascular disease with a
   Mediterranean diet supplemented with extra-virgin olive oil
   or nuts."
  N Engl J Med. 2018;378(25):e34.
  doi: 10.1056/NEJMoa1800389


----------------------------------------------------------------------
8. DII (Dietary Inflammatory Index) — 膳食炎症指数
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  【开山之作】
  Shivappa N, Steck SE, Hurley TG, Hussey JR, Hébert JR.
  "Designing and developing a literature-derived, population-based
   dietary inflammatory index."
  Public Health Nutr. 2014;17(8):1689-1696.
  doi: 10.1017/S1368980013002115


----------------------------------------------------------------------
9. DI-GM (Dietary Index for Gut Microbiota) — 肠道菌群膳食指数
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  【开山之作】
  Kase BE, Liese AD, Zhang J, Murphy EA, Zhao L, Steck SE.
  "The Development and Evaluation of a Literature-Based Dietary
   Index for Gut Microbiota."
  Nutrients. 2024;16(7):1045.
  doi: 10.3390/nu16071045


----------------------------------------------------------------------
10. PHDI (Planetary Health Diet Index) — 行星健康饮食指数
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  【开山之作】EAT-Lancet 委员会报告
  Willett W, Rockström J, Loken B, et al.
  "Food in the Anthropocene: the EAT-Lancet Commission on healthy
   diets from sustainable food systems."
  Lancet. 2019;393(10170):447-492.
  doi: 10.1016/S0140-6736(18)31788-4

  【PHDI 评分操作化】（本包PHDI函数实现）
  Cacau LT, De Carli E, de Carvalho AM, et al.
  "Development and Validation of an Index Based on EAT-Lancet
   Recommendations: The Planetary Health Diet Index."
  Nutrients. 2021;13(5):1698.
  doi: 10.3390/nu13051698


----------------------------------------------------------------------
11. ACS2020 (American Cancer Society 2020 Diet Score)
     — 美国癌症学会2020饮食评分
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  【ACS 2020 指南】
  Rock CL, Thomson C, Gansler T, et al.
  "American Cancer Society guideline for diet and physical
   activity for cancer prevention."
  CA Cancer J Clin. 2020;70(4):245-271.
  doi: 10.3322/caac.21591

  【ACS2020 评分开发】（本包ACS2020_V1/V2函数实现）
  McCullough ML, Chantaprasopsuk S, Islami F, et al.
  "Association of Socioeconomic and Geographic Factors With
   Diet Quality in US Adults."
  JAMA Netw Open. 2022;5(5):e2211618.
  doi: 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2022.11618
